Portland, Oregon

Marisol is 71. She takes a diuretic, which impairs her body's ability to shed heat.

Three times a week she walks to the pharmacy and the bus stop.

So the fastest route sends her down an exposed arterial, in full sun, with nowhere to sit down.

Shade isn't on the map.

So we put it there.

How it knows where the shade is.

No imagery and no weather model. Sun geometry and building footprints.

    What it does not know

    —Building heights are estimated from floor counts wherever OpenStreetMap has no height.

    —Tree coverage is only as complete as the map — an untagged street looks sunnier than it really is.

    —Shadows are convex hulls, and the ground is assumed flat.

    —Shade changes radiant temperature, not air temperature — which is why nothing here is quoted in degrees.
